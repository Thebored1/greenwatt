# About Green Watt

## Who We Are

Green Watt is an India-based company specialising in the supply, calibration support, and field application of electrical testing and measurement equipment. Headquartered in India, the company serves utilities, engineering contractors, and industrial clients who operate, maintain, or commission high-voltage and medium-voltage electrical infrastructure.

Founded by engineers with deep roots in the Indian power sector, Green Watt was established with a clear mission: to bring world-class electrical testing technology within reach of Indian organisations and to back every instrument sold with credible application knowledge and after-sales support.

## Our Mission

To enhance the safety, reliability, and efficiency of India's electrical infrastructure by supplying precision testing instruments and delivering expert on-site testing services — helping our clients prevent failures before they happen.

## What Sets Us Apart

**Technical Depth:** Our team comprises qualified electrical and instrumentation engineers who understand how the Indian grid operates, what standards apply (IS, IEC, CEA regulations), and how testing results translate into maintenance decisions.

**Trusted Global Principals:** Green Watt is an authorised partner for internationally recognised brands including:
- **Kingsine** — protection relay test systems (K3063i, K68i series)
- **OFIL Systems** — UV corona cameras and SF6 gas detection cameras
- **Satir** — thermal imaging cameras for electrical diagnostics
- **AB Chance** — electrical safety and live-line tools
- **Kisgen / Huaying** — transformer diagnostic and high-voltage test equipment

**Breadth of Portfolio:** From simple clamp meters to sophisticated SFRA (Sweep Frequency Response Analysis) systems and VLF cable testing sets, Green Watt covers the full diagnostic lifecycle of electrical assets.

**Field Services:** Beyond equipment supply, Green Watt's engineers carry out on-site testing assignments — partial discharge surveys, thermal imaging audits, earth integrity tests, relay calibration, and energy meter verification.

## Our Clients

Green Watt is proud to serve some of India's most important power sector organisations, including NTPC, NPCIL, NEEPCO, OHPC, L&T, Adani, Siemens, ABB, BSES, Indian Railways, Sterlite, Jindal, and multiple state transmission utilities.

## Our Commitment

Every instrument we supply is backed by application support, operator training, and service assistance. We believe that a testing instrument is only as valuable as the operator's ability to use it correctly — which is why technical guidance is embedded in everything we do.
