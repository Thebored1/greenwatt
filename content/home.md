# Green Watt — Electrical Testing Equipment & Services

## Your Trusted Partner in Electrical Testing Solutions

Green Watt is a leading Indian supplier of precision electrical testing equipment and on-site testing services, serving the power generation, transmission, and distribution sectors across India. With a comprehensive portfolio spanning energy meters, partial discharge detectors, thermal imagers, relay test systems, transformer diagnostic tools, and solar PV testers, Green Watt equips utilities, contractors, and industrial facilities with the instruments they need to maintain safe, reliable, and efficient electrical infrastructure.

## What We Do

Green Watt combines product supply with hands-on expertise. Our team of qualified electrical engineers sources and supports best-in-class instruments from globally recognised principals including Kingsine (relay test equipment), OFIL Systems (UV/corona and gas-detection cameras), Satir (thermal imaging), and AB Chance (safety products). Alongside equipment sales, we deliver on-site testing services — from routine energy meter calibration to advanced partial discharge surveys and thermal imaging audits.

- **Services:** Earth integrity testing, energy meter testing, relay testing, thermal imaging surveys, partial discharge detection
