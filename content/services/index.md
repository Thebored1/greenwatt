# Services — Electrical Testing & Inspection Services

## On-Site Electrical Testing Services by Green Watt

In addition to supplying electrical testing equipment, Green Watt provides professional on-site testing and inspection services delivered by qualified electrical engineers. Our field teams are equipped with the same high-quality instruments that we supply to clients — giving you confidence that your testing is performed with accurate, calibrated equipment operated by experienced professionals.

Green Watt's testing services are used by power utilities, transmission companies, industrial facilities, solar developers, and EPC contractors across India to meet statutory testing obligations, support planned maintenance programmes, and provide independent condition assessment of critical electrical assets.

## Our Services

### [Earth Integrity Testing](earth-integrity-testing.md)
Comprehensive earthing system testing covering earth resistance, soil resistivity, step and touch potential surveys, and earth electrode integrity verification.

### [Energy Meter Testing](energy-meter-testing.md)
On-site testing and verification of installed revenue-grade and industrial energy meters using portable reference standards, ensuring accuracy and compliance with CEA (Meters) Regulations.

### [Relay Testing](relay-testing.md)
On-site commissioning, periodic maintenance, and post-event testing of electromechanical, static, and numerical protection relays and IEDs.

### [Thermal Imaging Surveys](thermal-imaging-services.md)
Infrared thermography surveys of HV and LV switchgear, transformer connections, cable terminations, overhead lines, and solar PV arrays to identify thermal anomalies and hot spots before they cause failures.

### [Partial Discharge Detection](partial-discharge-detection.md)
On-site PD surveys of high-voltage cables, transformers, switchgear, and rotating machines using multi-sensor detection technology, identifying insulation defects without taking equipment out of service.
