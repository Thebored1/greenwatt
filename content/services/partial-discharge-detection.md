# Partial Discharge Detection

## On-Site Partial Discharge Survey Services

Partial discharge (PD) is a localised electrical breakdown within — or on the surface of — the insulation of a high-voltage asset. It is a proven precursor to insulation failure in cables, transformers, switchgear, and rotating machines. Crucially, PD can be detected long before failure occurs — giving asset owners the opportunity to take controlled corrective action rather than dealing with an emergency forced outage.

Green Watt's PD detection service uses multi-sensor measurement technology to survey energised HV equipment in service, identifying and locating PD sources without any interruption to supply.

## Types of Partial Discharge Detected

### Voids in Cable Insulation
Manufacturing defects or ageing-induced voids within XLPE cable insulation produce repetitive PD at specific phase angles. Green Watt engineers use HFCT (High Frequency Current Transformer) sensors clamped onto cable earth conductors to detect PD pulses propagating along the cable, locating the defect position by time-domain reflectometry.

### Floating Discharge
Metallic components within high-voltage equipment that are not properly bonded to earth can acquire a floating potential and produce intense, irregular PD. This is particularly common in switchgear spacers, connector hardware, and transformer internal components. Floating discharge is highly damaging and can lead to rapid insulation breakdown.

### Surface Tracking and Contamination Discharge
Contaminated or wet insulator surfaces in outdoor switchgear and cable sealing ends produce surface PD which, over time, causes carbon tracking and eventual flashover. TEV (Transient Earth Voltage) and acoustic sensors are particularly effective at detecting surface PD in switchgear.

## Measurement Techniques

- **HFCT (High Frequency Current Transformer)** — non-invasive clamp sensor on cable earth return
- **TEV (Transient Earth Voltage)** — surface contact sensor for metal-clad switchgear
- **UHF (Ultra High Frequency)** — coupler sensor for GIS, large power transformers
- **Acoustic/Ultrasonic** — airborne or contact sensor for locating PD sources by sound emission
- **Phase-Resolved PD Pattern (PRPD)** — diagnostic display for defect classification
