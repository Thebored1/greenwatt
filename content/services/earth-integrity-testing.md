# Earth Integrity Testing

## On-Site Earthing System Testing and Verification

A properly designed and maintained earthing system is the foundation of electrical safety in any substation, industrial facility, or commercial installation. Earth integrity testing verifies that the earthing network functions correctly — providing a low-impedance fault current return path, limiting touch and step potentials to safe levels, and ensuring that protective devices operate reliably during an earth fault. Green Watt's earth integrity testing service provides a comprehensive assessment of your earthing system using industry-standard methodologies.

## Tests Performed

### Earth Resistance Measurement
- Fall-of-potential (three-terminal) method for individual earth electrodes and earth mats
- Stakeless / clamp-on earth resistance testing for earth points within a multi-point earthing network
- Selective testing of individual earth electrodes within a combined earth grid

### Soil Resistivity Survey
- Wenner four-pin method across the site footprint
- Soil resistivity profile at different electrode spacings to determine soil layer structure
- Data used for earth grid design verification or remediation planning

### Earth Grid Integrity Verification
- Continuity testing of buried earth conductors and bonding connections
- Step and touch potential measurement at critical locations within and around the earth grid
- Comparison of measured values against IEC 60364, IS 3043, and IEEE Std 80 limits

### Neutral Earthing Resistor (NER) Testing
- Resistance and condition check of neutral earthing resistors
- High-current injection test for NER performance verification

## Why Earth Integrity Testing Matters

Poor earthing is responsible for a disproportionate number of fatal electrical accidents in India. Earthing systems deteriorate over time due to corrosion of buried conductors, changes in soil moisture, deterioration of joints, and physical damage — often invisible until a fault event reveals the deficiency. Proactive testing identifies problems before they become safety incidents.
