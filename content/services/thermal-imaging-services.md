# Thermal Imaging Services

## Infrared Thermography Surveys for Electrical Systems

Thermal imaging (infrared thermography) is one of the most cost-effective predictive maintenance techniques available for electrical infrastructure. By detecting heat generated by resistance increases, overloaded conductors, loose connections, and failing components — before any visible sign of a problem — a thermal survey can prevent unplanned outages, equipment damage, and fire risk. Green Watt's certified thermographers carry out thermal imaging surveys of HV and LV electrical equipment for utilities, industrial facilities, data centres, and commercial buildings across India.

## What We Survey

### High-Voltage Primary Equipment
- Power transformers — tank hotspots, bushing top connections, cooler pipework
- Transformer LV and HV cable sealing ends and gland plates
- 11 kV, 33 kV, and EHV outdoor switchgear — busbar connections, isolator contacts, CT secondary terminal boxes
- GIS (Gas Insulated Switchgear) — local hot spots on gas compartment enclosures

### LT and MV Secondary Equipment
- MV/LV switchboards and distribution boards — bus joints, cable lugs, circuit breaker connections
- Motor control centres (MCCs) — overloaded motor feeders, contactors, cable terminations
- Power factor correction (PFC) capacitor banks
- UPS and battery charger output terminals
- Control and protection relay panels — overloaded terminal blocks and wiring

### Overhead Lines and Structures
- Transmission and distribution line conductor joints and compression sleeves
- Overhead line insulator strings — pollution-induced heating
- Overhead earth wire connections

### Solar PV Arrays
- Module-level thermal survey for hot-cell defects, bypass diode failures, soiling patterns, and shading losses
- String combiner box connection thermal check

## Deliverables

Every thermal imaging survey produces a structured written report including:
- Thermal and visible-light image pairs for every anomaly found
- Measured and reference temperatures with delta-T values
- Severity classification (Immediate, Urgent, Routine) based on NETA/IS standards
- Identification of equipment, location, and recommended corrective action
- Priority ranking for maintenance scheduling
