# Energy Meter Testing

## On-Site Energy Meter Verification and Testing

Revenue energy meters are the financial instruments of the power sector — accuracy errors in installed meters directly translate to revenue loss or overcharging, with significant financial implications for both utilities and consumers. Green Watt provides on-site energy meter testing using portable reference standards, allowing metering teams to verify installed meter accuracy without removing meters from service.

## Service Scope

### In-Situ Accuracy Verification
Green Watt uses portable reference energy meters (single-phase and three-phase) calibrated to Class 0.05 accuracy to compare against the installed meter's reading under live load conditions. The test is performed at the meter's actual operating current and voltage, reflecting real-world accuracy rather than laboratory conditions.

**Meters Tested:**
- Single-phase electronic energy meters (LT residential and commercial consumers)
- Three-phase four-wire meters (industrial and HT consumers)
- Tri-vector meters (kWh, kVArh, kVAh, MD recording)
- Smart/AMI meters
- Panel meters and sub-meters

### CT/PT Ratio and Accuracy Chain Verification
For HT and EHV metering installations where the meter operates via measuring transformers, the accuracy of the entire metering chain (CT × PT × meter) is critical. Green Watt verifies the ratio accuracy and phase error of installed CTs and PTs as part of the metering verification exercise.

### Metering Panel and Secondary Wiring Check
- Verification of CT burden and connected secondary wiring resistance
- Check of PT burden and fuse conditions
- Terminal block connection integrity
- Phase sequence and polarity verification

## Applications

- Periodic statutory verification required by CEA (Meters) Regulations
- Consumer meter accuracy disputes — providing independent verification
- Post-installation commissioning check for new metering points
- Energy audit — metering accuracy as part of AT&C loss reduction programmes
- Industrial consumer metering verification

## Report

A detailed test report is provided for each metering point, including the tested meter identification, test conditions, reference meter details, measured accuracy at multiple load points, and pass/fail conclusion with respect to applicable accuracy class limits.
