# Contact Green Watt

## Get in Touch

Green Watt welcomes enquiries from utilities, EPC contractors, testing laboratories, industrial facilities, and individual engineers seeking electrical testing instruments, calibration services, or on-site testing support across India.

Whether you need a product quotation, technical application advice, a service proposal, or information about an upcoming training event or demonstration, our team is ready to assist.

## Enquiry Types

### Product Enquiries
Looking for a specific instrument or comparing options across our product range? Our technical sales team can advise on the right instrument for your application, arrange a product demonstration, and provide a formal quotation with delivery terms.

### Service Enquiries
For on-site testing services — earth integrity testing, energy meter verification, relay testing, thermal imaging surveys, or partial discharge detection — please describe your site location, the equipment to be tested, and your required timeline. We will respond with a service scope and fee proposal.

### After-Sales Support
For technical questions about instruments you have already purchased from Green Watt, calibration enquiries, spare parts, or service and repair requests, please contact our after-sales support team with your instrument model and serial number.

### General Enquiries
For all other enquiries, including business development, partnership discussions, and career opportunities, please use the contact form or reach us directly.
