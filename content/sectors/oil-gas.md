# Oil & Gas

## Electrical Testing for Oil & Gas Facilities

The oil and gas industry operates electrical systems in some of the most demanding and hazardous environments encountered by electrical engineers. Explosive atmospheres, corrosive conditions, remote locations, and the critical consequences of electrical failure — including fire, explosion, and environmental incident — mean that the highest standards of electrical testing and maintenance are essential. Green Watt supplies electrical testing instruments and delivers on-site testing services to upstream (exploration and production), midstream (pipelines and terminals), and downstream (refinery and petrochemical) oil and gas facilities across India.

## Hazardous Area Electrical Testing

All electrical equipment installed in Zone 0, Zone 1, and Zone 2 hazardous areas (classified per IEC 60079 / IS 5572) must be of approved Ex-rated design and maintained in accordance with IEC 60079-17. Electrical testing in these areas requires the use of intrinsically safe or approved instruments and strict adherence to permit-to-work and hot-work procedures.

- **Insulation resistance testing** of Ex-rated motors, cables, and distribution boards — using intrinsically safe or approved testers
- **Earth resistance and earth continuity testing** of equipment bonding and static dissipation systems — critical for preventing electrostatic ignition
- **Thermal imaging** of motor starters, MCC panels, and cable glands in non-sparking camera designs where applicable
- **Protection relay testing** for motor protection relays on critical process motors (compressors, pumps, fans)

## Substation and HV Equipment Maintenance

Onshore oil and gas facilities and offshore platforms have their own HV substation infrastructure requiring standard substation testing:

- Transformer diagnostics (winding resistance, TTR, oil BDV, SFRA, tan delta)
- Relay protection testing for incoming feeder and motor feeder protection
- CT/PT accuracy verification for revenue and process metering
- Switchgear insulation and partial discharge testing

## Pipeline and Cathodic Protection

- Earth resistance and soil resistivity measurements in support of cathodic protection system design and monitoring
- Continuity testing of pipeline isolating joints and flange connections
