# Data Centres

## Electrical Testing for Data Centre Critical Power Infrastructure

Data centres are among the most power-intensive facilities in the modern economy, and they are unique in their intolerance of electrical failure — even a few milliseconds of power interruption can cause data corruption, system crashes, and significant financial and reputational damage for the businesses they serve. The tiered reliability requirements of data centres (Uptime Institute Tier I through Tier IV) place stringent demands on the design, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance of all electrical systems.

Green Watt provides electrical testing products and services tailored to the critical power infrastructure of data centres — from initial commissioning through to ongoing maintenance and compliance testing.

## Power Infrastructure Testing

### HV/MV Supply and Transformer Testing
- Transformer winding resistance, turns ratio, and oil BDV testing during scheduled maintenance outages
- Partial discharge surveys of transformer bushings and 11 kV switchgear
- Thermal imaging of transformer LV terminals and MV switchgear

### UPS and Battery Systems
- **Battery internal resistance testing** — identifies ageing, failing, or open-circuit cells in VRLA and Li-ion UPS battery strings
- **Thermal imaging of battery banks** — high-resistance cells produce measurable heat, detectable before failure
- **UPS output power quality measurement** — voltage, frequency, THD (Total Harmonic Distortion), and unbalance
- **Static transfer switch (STS) testing**

### LV Distribution and Switchgear
- **Thermal imaging surveys** of all LV switchboards, PDUs (Power Distribution Units), busbars, and cable terminations — a standard data centre best practice on a 6-monthly or annual cycle
- **Insulation resistance testing** of power cables and submain circuits
- **Earth continuity testing** for equipotential bonding of server racks, raised floor, and cable management systems
- **Earth resistance testing** of building earthing system

### Generator Testing Support
- Insulation and winding resistance testing of standby generator sets
- Generator protection relay testing
- Thermal imaging of generator connections and AVR panels

## Compliance and Standards

Data centre electrical testing supports compliance with Uptime Institute tier classification, TIA-942, IS 14665 (fire safety), and IEC 60364 series for low-voltage electrical installations.
