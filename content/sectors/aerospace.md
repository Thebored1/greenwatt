# Aerospace

## Electrical Testing for Aerospace Facilities and Airport Infrastructure

The aerospace sector — including airports, airline maintenance bases, defence aviation facilities, and aerospace manufacturing plants — demands electrical testing to the very highest standards of accuracy and safety. Electrical failures in aerospace infrastructure can have consequences that extend far beyond the inconvenience of a utility outage; they directly affect the safety and continuity of flight operations.

Green Watt supplies precision electrical testing instruments and provides field testing services that meet the demanding requirements of the aerospace sector's electrical infrastructure.

## Applications in Aerospace Facilities

### Airport Electrical Infrastructure
Airports are large, complex electrical consumers with critical loads — including runway and apron lighting (Constant Current Regulators, series circuit lighting), air traffic control facilities, security systems, and passenger terminal systems — that must remain energised at all times.

- **Earth integrity testing** of airside and landside earthing systems, including aircraft earthing points and fuel hydrant bonding connections
- **Insulation testing** of underground cable circuits feeding airfield lighting
- **Energy meter testing** for utility metering at airport supply points
- **Thermal imaging** of HV/LV transformer installations, distribution boards, and UPS systems in terminal buildings

### Aerospace Manufacturing and MRO Facilities
Aircraft manufacturing and maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) facilities operate large CNC machines, precision test equipment, cleanrooms, and environmental test chambers — all requiring reliable, clean power.

- **Power quality measurement** to identify harmonics, voltage sags, and transients affecting sensitive manufacturing equipment
- **Partial discharge surveys** of HV cable systems and switchgear in manufacturing plants
- **Relay protection testing** of HV substation protection for manufacturing facility power supply
- **Insulation testing** of motor windings in large HVAC and process equipment

### Defence Aviation Bases
- Electrical testing of ground power units (GPUs) and aircraft ground power connections
- Earthing and bonding verification for aircraft refuelling areas
- Substation maintenance testing for critical defence infrastructure
