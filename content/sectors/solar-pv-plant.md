# Solar PV Plants

## Electrical Testing for Utility-Scale and Commercial Solar PV Plants

India is one of the world's fastest-growing solar energy markets, with ambitious capacity targets driving large-scale PV plant development across the country. Rigorous electrical testing at commissioning, and ongoing performance monitoring during operation, are essential to ensure that solar plants generate the energy they are designed to produce and operate safely throughout their 25-year design life.

Green Watt supplies solar-specific testing instruments and provides field testing services for solar PV plants — from rooftop systems to utility-scale ground-mounted arrays.

## Testing at Commissioning

### String and Array Verification (IEC 62446-1)
- Open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current measurement per string to verify correct module count and identify shading, polarity reversal, or failed bypass diodes
- Insulation resistance testing of each string using bipolar DC insulation testers — positive-to-earth and negative-to-earth measurements without disconnecting the array
- Continuity of protective earth conductors
- String combiner box termination thermal check

### I-V Curve Tracing
- I-V and P-V curve measurement per string or sub-array using the MCM1600PV analyser
- STC-normalised curve comparison against manufacturer's datasheet to identify underperforming strings
- Maximum power point tracking (MPPT) range verification

### DC System Testing
- Cable insulation resistance and earth fault detection using portable DC insulation testers
- DC fuse and string protection device continuity check
- DC array cable thermal imaging

### Inverter Connection and Protection Verification
- Anti-islanding protection test
- Inverter protection relay (overvoltage, undervoltage, overfrequency) verification

## Ongoing O&M Testing

- Periodic thermal imaging surveys of PV modules to detect hot cells, delamination, and soiling patterns
- Annual insulation resistance check of DC strings
- DC string current monitoring using MDLA-100A-DC data loggers
- Performance ratio trending and energy yield analysis
