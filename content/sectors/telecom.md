# Telecom & Communications

## Electrical Testing for Telecommunications Infrastructure

India's telecommunications network — spanning mobile towers, optical fibre landing stations, internet exchange points, and broadcast transmission facilities — relies on robust, reliable electrical infrastructure. Power is the lifeblood of telecom: a single power failure can disrupt communications for millions of users across a wide area. Green Watt provides electrical testing instruments and field services that help telecom infrastructure operators maintain power reliability and meet statutory safety requirements.

## Key Testing Requirements in Telecom

### Tower Earthing and Lightning Protection
Telecom towers are tall, exposed structures that attract lightning strikes. The earthing and bonding system must safely dissipate lightning energy and maintain equipment protection. Tower earthing systems are subject to corrosion, vandalism, and gradual deterioration.

- **Earth resistance measurement** at each tower base using the fall-of-potential method
- **Clamp-on earth resistance testing** for quick periodic checks without disconnecting the system
- **Lightning protection continuity testing** — earth conductor, down conductor, and air termination bonds
- **Soil resistivity survey** for earthing system design or upgrade projects
- Compliance with IS 3043 and TEC earthing standards for telecom infrastructure

### Battery and DC Power Systems
Telecom sites typically use 48 V DC power systems with VRLA or lithium battery banks for backup power.

- **DC battery system testing** — internal resistance measurement, capacity testing
- **Thermal imaging** of rectifier/charger units and battery banks to identify cells with elevated temperature
- **Insulation testing** of DC feeders and equipment

### Substation and Grid Connection Testing
Larger telecom facilities (data-carrying hubs, exchange buildings, landing stations) connect to the grid at 11 kV or 33 kV and have their own substation equipment.

- **Transformer maintenance testing** — winding resistance, oil BDV, ratio
- **Relay protection testing** for incoming feeder and transformer protection
- **Thermal imaging** of HV/LV switchgear
