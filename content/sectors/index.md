# Sectors — Industries Served by Green Watt

## Electrical Testing Solutions Across Key Indian Industries

Green Watt's products and services are applied across a wide range of industries where reliable, safe electrical infrastructure is critical. From the generation bays of large power stations to the antenna structures of telecom towers, our instruments and field engineers support the complete spectrum of Indian electrical infrastructure.

## Sectors We Serve

### [Power Substations](power-substations.md)
Transmission and distribution substations from 11 kV to 765 kV — relay testing, transformer diagnostics, CT/PT verification, partial discharge surveys, and thermal imaging.

### [Solar PV Plants](solar-pv-plant.md)
Utility-scale and commercial solar PV plants — I-V curve tracing, insulation testing, thermal imaging of arrays, and DC string monitoring.

### [Transmission Lines](transmission-line.md)
EHV overhead transmission lines — thermal imaging of conductor joints, corona detection using UV cameras, and relay protection testing at associated substations.

### [Oil & Gas](oil-gas.md)
Onshore and offshore oil and gas facilities — hazardous area electrical equipment testing, insulation testing of Ex-rated motors and cables, and thermography of MCC and switchgear.

### [Data Centres](data-center.md)
Critical power infrastructure in data centres — UPS system testing, power quality measurement, switchgear thermography, and earth integrity testing.

### [Telecom & Communications](telecom.md)
Telecom towers, exchange buildings, and data transmission facilities — earthing system testing, insulation resistance testing of power feeders, and battery backup system checks.

### [Aerospace](aerospace.md)
Airport ground power systems and aerospace facility electrical infrastructure — precision electrical testing to the demanding safety standards of the aviation industry.

## Cross-Sector Capabilities

Certain Green Watt products and services are valued across all sectors:

- Thermal imaging surveys apply wherever there are energised electrical connections that can degrade
- Earth integrity testing is required at every type of facility
- Insulation testing is fundamental to the maintenance of any electrical system
- Energy meter verification is relevant wherever metered energy is bought or sold
