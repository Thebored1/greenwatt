# Transmission Lines

## Electrical Testing for EHV Overhead Transmission Infrastructure

India's extra-high-voltage (EHV) transmission network — operated primarily by PGCIL (Power Grid Corporation of India) and state transmission utilities — spans hundreds of thousands of circuit-kilometres of 66 kV to 765 kV overhead lines. Maintaining the integrity of this vast network requires specialised diagnostic tools that can identify developing faults on energised lines without shutdown — and protection testing tools that verify the relay systems which clear faults when they do occur.

Green Watt supplies transmission-specialist instruments and supports transmission utilities with field testing services across the Indian grid.

## Line Patrol and Condition Monitoring

### Thermal Imaging of Overhead Lines
Resistance heating in conductor joints, compression sleeves, ACSR/ACCC conductor connections, and clamp-type hardware causes localised hot spots that reduce current-carrying capacity and, if left unchecked, can lead to conductor annealing and sag, or joint burndown.

- Airborne thermal imaging surveys using helicopter or drone-mounted IR cameras for rapid patrol of long line sections
- Ground-level thermal imaging using high-resolution long-range telephoto IR cameras (YRH600 and similar models) from road-accessible tower locations
- Identification and quantification of thermal anomalies at joints, strain clamps, spacer dampers, and earth wire connections

### UV Corona Detection
Corona discharge on energised HV hardware, insulator strings, and conductors produces radio-frequency interference, ozone, and (over time) surface degradation. OFIL DayCor UV cameras allow solar-blind corona detection in daylight:

- Ground-based UV patrol for up to 765 kV transmission towers from standoff distance
- Helicopter-borne UV survey capability for rapid long-line inspection
- Quantitative corona discharge intensity measurement for severity assessment

## Protection Testing at Transmission Substations

Transmission line protection relies on distance protection (impedance relay), current differential protection (pilot fibre), and teleprotection schemes — all of which require precise periodic testing using advanced relay test equipment such as the Kingsine K3063i.

- End-to-end distance relay zone reach and time verification
- Pilot protection scheme testing (POTT, DUTT, blocking schemes)
- Auto-reclose dead-time and synchronism-check function testing
- Power swing blocking and out-of-step tripping function verification

## Compliance

Testing supports Grid Code compliance, CEA (Technical Standards) regulations, and PGCIL/STU maintenance procedures.
