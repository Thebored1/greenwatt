# Winding Resistance Tester

## Green Watt DC Winding Resistance Test Set

DC winding resistance measurement is an essential diagnostic test for power transformers, distribution transformers, motors, and generators. It reveals turn-to-turn short circuits, loose or corroded tap-changer contacts, broken conductor strands, and high-resistance joints — defects that cause heating, inefficiency, and eventual failure. Green Watt's winding resistance tester delivers fast, accurate DC resistance measurement with automatic demagnetisation, minimising test time on large power transformer windings.

## Key Features

- **High test current output** — multiple current ranges (typically 1 A, 10 A, 100 A) for measuring resistance on both low-resistance power transformer windings and higher-resistance distribution transformer LV windings
- **Wide resistance measurement range** — from micro-ohms (µΩ) to kilo-ohms (kΩ) in a single instrument, covering all winding types
- **Fast stabilisation** — regulated DC current source achieves steady-state quickly even on highly inductive windings, reducing test time compared with conventional battery-based methods
- **Simultaneous three-phase measurement** — some models test all three phases simultaneously, greatly accelerating testing on large three-phase transformer banks
- **Automatic demagnetisation** — controlled current reversal removes residual core magnetism at the end of the test, protecting the transformer from inrush current damage on re-energisation
- **Tap-changer testing** — continuous resistance monitoring during OLTC (On-Load Tap Changer) operation detects contact resistance spikes and transition resistance anomalies
- **Temperature correction** — automatic resistance correction to reference temperature (75°C or 20°C) using measured or entered winding temperature
- **Data storage and reporting** — internal memory with USB download and test report generation
- **Colour touchscreen display** — real-time resistance curve display during stabilisation phase

## Applications

- Commissioning and maintenance of power and distribution transformers
- On-load tap changer (OLTC) dynamic resistance testing — a critical maintenance check for OLTC contact wear
- Motor and generator winding resistance measurement for imbalance detection
- Reactor and inductor winding resistance verification
- Post-repair verification of rewound transformers

## Compliance

Test methodology complies with IEC 60076-1, IS 2026, and IEEE C57.12.90 winding resistance measurement requirements.
