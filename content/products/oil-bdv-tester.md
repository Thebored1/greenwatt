# Oil BDV Tester — Transformer Oil Breakdown Voltage Tester

## Automatic Transformer Oil BDV Test Set

The dielectric breakdown voltage (BDV) of transformer insulating oil is a primary indicator of oil quality and contamination level. Moisture, particulate contamination, and oxidation all degrade the oil's dielectric strength — reducing the insulation margin of the transformer and increasing the risk of internal flashover. Green Watt's automatic oil BDV tester performs the oil breakdown test to IEC 60156 / IS 335 standards, giving maintenance engineers a reliable, repeatable assessment of oil condition in the field or workshop.

## Key Features

- **Fully automatic test cycle** — automatic electrode gap setting, oil stirring, voltage ramping, breakdown detection, and result calculation
- **Test voltage:** up to 100 kV AC (model dependent)
- **IEC 60156 compliant** — standard test method with controlled ramp rate (2 kV/s)
- **Six-shot automatic test sequence** — automatically performs 6 breakdown tests and calculates the mean BDV in kV
- **Motorised oil stirrer** — ensures uniform mixing of the test sample between shots to disperse any carbon particles from the previous breakdown
- **Safety interlocks** — high-voltage enclosure with door interlock; automatic discharge after each shot
- **Automatic report generation** — test results with date, time, operator, and sample identification stored internally and exportable via USB / thermal printer
- **Large colour display** — real-time voltage ramp display and breakdown waveform capture
- **Portable carry case** — suitable for workshop and site use in transformer yards and oil processing facilities

## Test Cell

- Standard IEC 60156 test cell with 2.5 mm spherical electrodes
- Borosilicate glass oil cup with minimum 350 ml fill volume
- Easy disassembly and cleaning between samples

## Applications

- Routine condition monitoring of transformer insulating oil per preventive maintenance schedules
- Acceptance testing of new transformer oil on delivery
- Post-degassing and oil filtration quality verification
- Fault investigation — oil quality check following a transformer incident
- Oil reclamation plant quality control

## Compliance

Tests are conducted in accordance with IEC 60156, IS 335, and ASTM D877/D1816 standards, meeting the requirements of CEA (Technical Standards for Construction, Operation and Maintenance of Electrical Plants and Electric Lines) regulations.
