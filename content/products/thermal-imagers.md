# Thermal Imagers — Infrared Cameras for Electrical Diagnostics

## Green Watt Thermal Imaging Range

Thermal imaging is the most efficient method of identifying hot spots, overloaded connections, failing components, and insulation weaknesses in energised electrical systems — all without contact and without interrupting supply. Green Watt supplies a range of infrared cameras sourced from Satir and other leading manufacturers, covering entry-level handheld models through to high-resolution professional systems used in substation and industrial audits.

## Models Available

### CK350
A professional-grade infrared camera with high thermal sensitivity, designed for detailed electrical and mechanical inspections in substations and industrial plants.

**Specifications (typical):**
- Detector resolution: 320 × 240 or better
- Thermal sensitivity (NETD): ≤ 50 mK
- Temperature range: −20°C to +650°C
- Built-in visible-light camera for fusion imaging
- Image storage: internal memory + SD card

### EP3501
Mid-range thermal imager combining compact ergonomics with high performance. Well suited to distribution substation surveys, panel inspections, and motor condition monitoring.

**Key Features:**
- Real-time image fusion (thermal + visible)
- Min/max/average spot temperature markers
- Isotherm and alarm threshold setting
- Rugged IP54-rated housing

### G96
A compact, cost-effective infrared camera for routine electrical maintenance inspections. Ideal for teams conducting frequent patrol surveys across LT and HT distribution networks.

**Key Features:**
- Lightweight and ergonomic one-hand operation
- 9 Hz refresh rate with clear thermal images
- Temperature range up to 350°C
- USB connectivity for report generation

### YRH600
A high-resolution thermal camera for demanding diagnostic tasks including transformer tank surveys, high-voltage bushing inspection, and overhead line hot-spot detection.

**Key Features:**
- High detector resolution for long-distance spot detection
- Wide temperature range and interchangeable lenses
- Advanced image analysis software included
- Suitable for 33 kV to 765 kV outdoor inspection

### HRyxj-a and Mini-R Series
Compact and semi-professional thermal modules and handheld cameras for integration into inspection workflows where portability is paramount.

## Applications

- Substation primary equipment inspection (transformers, busbars, cable terminations)
- LT panel, motor control centre (MCC), and switchboard surveys
- Overhead transmission and distribution line patrol
- Motor bearing and mechanical hot-spot detection
- Solar PV panel thermal survey for cell defects
