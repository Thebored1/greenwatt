# Clamp Meters — Current Measurement Instruments

## Green Watt Clamp Meters

Clamp meters allow current measurement on live conductors without breaking the circuit — an essential safety and convenience feature for electrical maintenance and commissioning work. Green Watt supplies a range of clamp meters from standard models for everyday electrical work through to heavy-duty monster-jaw clamps for large busbar and cable bundle applications.

## Models Available

### Green Watt Standard Clamp Meter
A professional-grade, true-RMS clamp meter suitable for measurement on standard single-core conductors and cables in LT switchgear, motor control centres, and distribution boards.

**Key Features:**
- True-RMS AC current measurement up to 600 A or 1000 A
- AC/DC voltage measurement up to 600 V / 1000 V
- Jaw opening: 40 mm — covers standard cables and busbars up to 400 A
- Resistance, continuity, diode test, and capacitance measurement
- Frequency and duty cycle measurement
- Auto-ranging with data hold and max/min capture
- CAT III 600 V / CAT IV 300 V safety rating
- Backlit display for use in dimly lit panels and switchrooms

### Green Watt Monster Jaw Clamp Meter
The Monster Jaw Clamp Meter is engineered for measurement on large-diameter cables, multiple bundled cables, and large busbar sections commonly found in main incomer panels, transformer LV terminals, and industrial power distribution.

**Key Features:**
- Extra-large jaw opening: 85 mm or greater — accommodates large busbars and bundled cable configurations
- True-RMS AC current: up to 3000 A
- AC/DC voltage: up to 1000 V
- Inrush current capture mode for motor starting current measurement
- Low-pass filter mode for accurate measurements in VFD/inverter environments
- Bluetooth data transmission to smartphone app (model dependent)
- Robust, drop-resistant housing
- CAT IV 600 V safety rating for use at the service entrance level

## Applications

- Transformer LV terminal and feeder current measurement
- Substation battery charger and DCDB current monitoring
- Motor load current verification and imbalance checking
- Energy audit and load survey work
- Solar PV string current measurement (DC clamp models available)
- Large industrial busbar current measurement without panel shutdown
