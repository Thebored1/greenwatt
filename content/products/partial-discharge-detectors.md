# Partial Discharge Detectors

## Detect Insulation Defects Before They Cause Failures

Partial discharge (PD) is one of the most reliable early indicators of insulation degradation in high-voltage cables, transformers, switchgear, and rotating machines. Green Watt supplies a range of partial discharge detection instruments — from entry-level analogue detectors to sophisticated multi-channel digital systems — enabling maintenance teams to locate and quantify PD activity without taking equipment out of service.

## Models Available

### Penta-PD Partial Discharge Detector
The Penta-PD is a multi-sensor, multi-channel PD detection system designed for comprehensive on-site PD surveys of GIS (Gas Insulated Switchgear), power transformers, and cable terminations. It combines acoustic, UHF, HFCT (High Frequency Current Transformer), and transient earth voltage (TEV) sensing modalities in a single portable platform.

**Key Features:**
- Five simultaneous measurement channels
- UHF, TEV, acoustic (AE/ultrasonic), and HFCT sensor inputs
- Phase-resolved partial discharge (PRPD) pattern display for defect classification
- GPS-tagged measurement logging for site mapping
- Wireless sensor option for remote or hazardous locations
- Rechargeable battery with minimum 8 hours field operation

### Digital Partial Discharge Detector
A single-channel or dual-channel digital PD detector suitable for routine condition monitoring of cables, bushings, and transformers. Offers digital signal processing for improved noise rejection in electrically noisy substation environments.

**Key Features:**
- Narrowband and wideband detection modes
- Pulse-height analysis and PRPD pattern capture
- Calibration pulse injection for apparent charge (pC) calibration per IEC 60270
- Compact handheld form factor with touch screen interface
- Data export via USB and optional Bluetooth

### Analogue Partial Discharge Detector
A field-proven analogue PD detector for basic insulation surveys. Provides fast, intuitive indication of PD presence and magnitude — well suited for quick patrol inspections of cable joints, transformer bushings, and switchgear.

**Key Features:**
- Broadband detection with adjustable frequency window
- Analogue meter and audio output for rapid assessment
- Lightweight and robust for frequent field use
- Compatible with standard coupling capacitors and HFCT probes

## Applications

- Condition assessment of XLPE and PILC power cables
- Transformer bushing and winding insulation surveys
- GIS compartment integrity checks
- Rotating machine (motor and generator) stator insulation monitoring
