# Insulation Testers — Insulation Resistance Test Instruments

## Green Watt Insulation Testers

Insulation resistance testing is one of the most fundamental preventive maintenance checks performed on electrical equipment. A drop in insulation resistance gives early warning of moisture ingress, contamination, ageing, and physical damage — all of which can lead to earth faults, flashovers, and equipment failures. Green Watt supplies a range of insulation resistance testers (megohmmeters) covering standard electrical equipment, solar PV systems, and high-resistance applications.

## Models Available

### Standard Insulation Tester (GW Series)
A robust, portable megohmmeter for general-purpose insulation resistance testing of cables, motors, transformers, switchgear, and distribution boards.

**Key Features:**
- Test voltages: 250 V, 500 V, 1000 V, 2500 V, 5000 V (model dependent)
- Measurement range: up to 10 TΩ
- PI (Polarisation Index) and DAR (Dielectric Absorption Ratio) automatic calculation
- Step voltage test and ramp voltage test modes for cable insulation assessment
- Large backlit display with analogue bar graph
- Auto-discharge circuit for safe release of stored charge after testing
- CAT IV safety rating
- Data logging with USB download

### Solar PV Insulation Tester (GW-PV Series)
Specifically designed for testing insulation resistance of solar photovoltaic arrays, where the DC floating nature of PV strings creates unique measurement challenges. The GW-PV tester handles the bipolar PV array topology safely.

**Key Features:**
- Safe bipolar measurement without disconnecting the array
- Test voltage up to 1000 V DC (or 1500 V DC for high-voltage PV systems)
- Automatic string polarity detection
- Simultaneous measurement of positive-to-earth and negative-to-earth insulation
- Minimum insulation resistance alarm threshold setting (per IEC 62446)
- Temperature-compensated readings

### PVS Insulation Tester
An enhanced solar PV tester combining insulation resistance measurement with I-V curve tracing capability — allowing field engineers to perform both insulation and performance testing of PV strings in a single visit.

### High-Resistance IR Tester (GW-IR)
For high-voltage cable and transformer insulation measurement where very high test voltages and ultra-high resistance ranges are required.

## Applications

- Motor and generator winding insulation assessment
- Power cable and cable joint insulation testing
- Transformer winding insulation before energisation
- Solar PV system commissioning and maintenance per IEC 62446
- Switchgear and busbar insulation checks
