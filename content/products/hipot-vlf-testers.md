# Hipot & VLF Testers — High-Voltage Cable and Equipment Testing

## High-Potential (Hipot) and VLF Test Systems

High-voltage withstand testing is used to verify the dielectric integrity of cable insulation, transformer windings, switchgear, and other HV apparatus — both at commissioning and as part of periodic maintenance programmes. Green Watt supplies AC hipot, DC hipot, and VLF (Very Low Frequency) test systems, offering utilities and contractors the right high-voltage testing tool for every application.

## Models Available

### AC Hipot Tester
AC withstand voltage testing applies power-frequency (50 Hz) alternating voltage to the test object at a level significantly above normal operating voltage. This is the most representative test for in-service AC insulation stress.

**Key Features:**
- Output voltage: 5 kV to 100 kV AC (model dependent)
- Precise voltage control with smooth ramp-up
- Leakage current measurement and trip threshold setting
- Partial discharge monitoring input (optional)
- Lightweight resonant design for larger capacitive loads (cables, GIS)
- Automatic pass/fail indication

### DC Hipot Tester
DC high-potential testing provides a sensitive measure of insulation leakage current and is particularly useful for assessing XLPE and EPR cable insulation condition. Lower test voltage levels than AC testing reduce the risk of creating new defects during the test.

**Key Features:**
- Output voltage: up to 80 kV DC (model dependent)
- Ultra-low ripple DC output for accurate leakage current measurement
- Leakage current profile display — step voltage and time-step test modes
- PI/DAR calculation from leakage current trends
- Built-in automatic discharge with residual voltage indicator
- Digital display of applied voltage and leakage current simultaneously

### VLF (Very Low Frequency) Cable Test Set
VLF testing at 0.1 Hz applies an alternating voltage at a fraction of power frequency, dramatically reducing the reactive current demand compared with 50 Hz AC testing — making it practical to test long HV cable circuits with compact, portable equipment.

**Key Features:**
- Test frequency: 0.1 Hz (VLF sinusoidal or cosine-rectangular waveform)
- Output voltage: up to 72 kV (for 33 kV XLPE cables) or 50 kV (for 11 kV cables)
- Tan delta (dielectric loss) measurement at VLF frequency for cable condition assessment
- Partial discharge measurement during VLF test (combined VLF-PD systems)
- Single-operator portable design
- Battery operation for site use without mains power

## Applications

- Factory and site acceptance testing of HV cables and cable systems
- Post-repair hipot testing of cable joints and terminations
- Transformer and switchgear withstand testing
- Pre-commissioning testing of GIS (Gas Insulated Switchgear)
- Periodic dielectric testing of aged cable circuits
