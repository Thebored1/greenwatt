# Gas Detection Cameras — SF6 Leak Detection & UV Corona Cameras

## Optical Gas Imaging and Corona Detection Cameras

Green Watt supplies advanced optical cameras for two critical but invisible electrical hazards: SF6 gas leaks in gas-insulated switchgear and circuit breakers, and corona discharge on overhead lines and high-voltage equipment. These cameras — sourced from OFIL Systems (UV corona) and Satir (thermal/gas) — allow operators to locate leaks and discharge activity quickly, safely, and without taking equipment out of service.

## SF6 Gas Detection Camera

SF6 (sulphur hexafluoride) is the insulating and arc-quenching medium used in GIS substations, ring main units, and SF6 circuit breakers throughout the Indian transmission and distribution network. SF6 is a potent greenhouse gas — even small leaks must be located and repaired promptly to comply with environmental regulations and to maintain equipment dielectric integrity.

### Thermal Gas Detection Camera (Satir / OFIL)
Uses infrared optical gas imaging (OGI) technology to make invisible SF6 gas plumes visible in real time — the leaking gas cloud appears as a dark smoke-like image against the thermal background.

**Key Features:**
- Cooled MCT (Mercury Cadmium Telluride) IR detector for SF6 absorption band detection
- Real-time video display of gas leak plumes
- No equipment shutdown required — survey in service
- Simultaneous standard visible-light and thermal-IR image overlay
- Video recording for documentation and reporting
- Detects SF6 leaks down to very low leak rates (grams per year)
- Also detects: hydrocarbon gases, CO₂, and other refrigerant gases (depending on filter)

## UV Corona Detection Camera (OFIL Systems)

Corona discharge on transmission lines, insulators, corona rings, and cable terminations produces ultraviolet (UV) light not visible to the naked eye. Left undetected, corona causes radio frequency interference, ozone production, nitric acid attack on insulators, and eventual insulation flashover.

### OFIL DayCor® Series UV Cameras
OFIL Systems is a world leader in UV corona detection cameras. Their solar-blind UV cameras operate in the 240–280 nm band — a range where solar background radiation is absent, allowing corona detection in full daylight conditions.

**Key Features:**
- Solar-blind UV detection — fully operational in bright daylight
- Simultaneous UV and visible-light fusion image display
- Quantification of corona discharge intensity (UV photon count)
- Zoom lenses for inspection of transmission towers from ground level
- Suitable for helicopter-borne surveys of EHV transmission lines
- Video and image data logging with GPS tagging

## Applications

- GIS substation SF6 leak surveys
- Ring main unit (RMU) and SF6 CB leak detection
- Transmission line corona survey (insulators, conductors, fittings, spacers)
- Cable sealing end and surge arrester corona inspection
- Environmental compliance monitoring of SF6-insulated equipment
