# Energy Meters — On-Site Reference Energy Meters

## Green Watt Reference Energy Meters

Green Watt supplies on-site reference energy meters for the verification and calibration of revenue-grade and industrial energy meters in the field. These portable reference standards allow metering engineers and utility inspection teams to test installed meters without removing them from service, significantly reducing downtime and logistical cost.

## Models Available

### 1-Phase On-Site Energy Meter Reference (GW-EMR1)
A compact, portable single-phase reference energy meter designed for field testing of residential, commercial, and light-industrial energy meters. Ideal for distribution companies (DISCOMs) and meter testing laboratories conducting in-situ verification.

**Key Features:**
- High-accuracy reference standard (Class 0.05 or better)
- Portable, battery-operated for field use
- Direct connection or clamp-on current input options
- Large backlit display with real-time power, energy, power factor, and frequency readout
- Data logging and PC interface for test record management
- Complies with IS 16444 / IEC 62053 accuracy requirements

### 3-Phase On-Site Energy Meter Reference (GW-EMR3)
A three-phase portable reference energy meter for testing poly-phase meters on distribution transformers, industrial feeders, and HT consumer installations.

**Key Features:**
- Three-phase four-wire and three-wire measurement modes
- Class 0.05 accuracy across the full current and voltage range
- Simultaneous display of all three phases — voltage, current, power factor, active and reactive energy
- Built-in current clamps for non-intrusive CT secondary measurement
- RS-232 / USB communication for automated test reporting
- Rugged housing suitable for substation and outdoor environments

## Applications

- Revenue meter verification and audit by utility inspection teams
- Post-installation commissioning check of LT and HT energy meters
- Periodic statutory verification per CEA (Meters) Regulations
- Energy audit and loss-reduction programmes by DISCOMs and state electricity boards

## Why Green Watt Energy Meters

Green Watt's reference energy meters are built to operate in the demanding thermal and electrical environment of Indian substations and feeder pillars, with wide temperature tolerance and robust EMI immunity — ensuring reliable readings even in the presence of harmonics and switching transients.
