# Products — Electrical Testing Equipment

## Green Watt Product Portfolio

Green Watt supplies a comprehensive range of electrical testing and diagnostic instruments designed for utilities, contractors, and industrial operators working across the Indian power sector. Our products span every stage of the asset lifecycle — commissioning, routine maintenance, predictive diagnostics, and fault investigation.

All instruments are sourced from globally certified manufacturers and are supported by Green Watt's in-house application engineers.

## Product Categories

### Metering & Power Quality
- [Energy Meters](energy-meters.md) — 1-phase and 3-phase on-site reference energy meters (GW-EMR3 and single-phase variant)
- [Clamp Meters](clamp-meters.md) — Standard and monster-jaw clamp meters for current measurement in tight or large-busbar installations
- [Multimeter](multimeter.md) — Professional-grade digital multimeters
