# Tan Delta Tester — Insulation Power Factor & Tan Delta Test System

## Green Watt Tan Delta (Dissipation Factor) Test Set

The tan delta (dielectric dissipation factor, or insulation power factor) test is one of the most sensitive diagnostic tests available for assessing the condition of transformer main insulation, bushing insulation, cable insulation, and rotating machine windings. Unlike simple insulation resistance testing, the tan delta measurement detects distributed moisture, contamination, and ageing in insulation even before there is a measurable drop in insulation resistance — providing earlier warning of deterioration and enabling proactive maintenance decisions.

## Principle of Operation

When an AC voltage is applied to a capacitive insulation system, a small portion of the current flows in phase with the voltage (resistive component) rather than 90° leading (ideal capacitive component). The tangent of the loss angle (δ) — tan δ — quantifies this dielectric loss. An increase in tan δ over successive measurements indicates insulation degradation. The absolute value and its voltage dependence (tip-up test) together give a comprehensive picture of insulation health.

## Key Features

- **High-voltage output:** up to 12 kV, 28 kV, or higher (model dependent) for testing at or above normal service voltage stress
- **Capacitance and tan delta measurement:** simultaneous automatic calculation
- **Grounded specimen (UST) and ungrounded specimen (GST) test modes** — covers both directly grounded and floating test objects
- **Guard terminal** for eliminating stray surface leakage currents from measurements
- **Voltage dependency (tip-up) test** — automatic multi-voltage test from lower voltage up to rated test voltage, displaying tan δ as a function of voltage
- **Frequency variation test** — some models offer variable frequency (45–65 Hz) for additional diagnostic information
- **Temperature logging** — ambient and surface temperature recording for correction to reference temperature
- **High accuracy** — tan δ resolution of 0.1 × 10⁻⁴ or better
- **Automated report generation** — tabular and graphical results with pass/fail assessment against IEEE C57.12.90, IEC 60137, and IEC 60243 limits

## Applications

- Power transformer main insulation and bushing tan delta at commissioning and during maintenance
- Generator and motor stator winding insulation assessment
- High-voltage cable insulation condition monitoring
- Current transformer and voltage transformer insulation testing
- Surge arrester and capacitor bank insulation checks
