# CT/PT Analyzer — Current & Potential Transformer Test System

## Green Watt CT/PT Analyzer

The Green Watt CT/PT Analyzer is a fully automated, portable instrument for the comprehensive testing of current transformers (CTs) and potential transformers (PTs/VTs) installed in metering and protection circuits. Accurate CT and PT performance is fundamental to revenue metering integrity and protection relay dependability — the CT/PT Analyzer allows engineers to verify transformer parameters on site, at the equipment's installed location, without the need for a laboratory.

## Key Tests Performed

### Current Transformer (CT) Tests
- **Ratio accuracy and phase displacement** — verification of declared accuracy class per IS 2705 / IEC 61869-2
- **Excitation (magnetisation) curve** — knee-point voltage (Vk) and exciting current measurement for protection class CTs
- **Burden measurement** — connected burden at rated secondary current
- **Winding resistance** — DC resistance of primary and secondary windings
- **Polarity check** — confirmation of terminal markings

### Potential Transformer (PT/VT) Tests
- **Voltage ratio and phase error** — across the full burden and voltage range
- **Magnetising current and excitation curve**
- **Burden and power factor of the secondary circuit**
- **Winding resistance**
- **Polarity verification**

## Key Features

- Fully automated test sequence with one-button operation
- Wide CT ratio range covering protection and metering CTs from distribution to EHV
- High-voltage excitation source built in — no external HV supply required
- Automatic data storage with time-stamped test reports
- USB and RS-232 output for PC-based report generation
- Colour touch-screen display with graphical excitation curve plotting
- Lightweight and battery-powered for prolonged field use
- Meets IEC 61869, IS 2705, and IEEE C57.13 test requirements

## Applications

- New CT/PT commissioning in 11 kV to 400 kV substations
- Periodic statutory testing of revenue metering CTs and PTs
- Post-fault investigation of protection CT saturation events
- Acceptance testing of CT/PT batches at manufacturer's works
