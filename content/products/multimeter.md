# Multimeter — Professional Digital Multimeters

## Green Watt Digital Multimeters

A high-quality digital multimeter (DMM) is the most frequently used instrument in any electrical maintenance toolkit. Green Watt supplies professional-grade multimeters engineered for the demanding environment of substation, industrial, and field electrical work — combining measurement accuracy, safety-rated inputs, and durable construction.

## Key Features

- **True-RMS measurement** — accurate voltage and current readings in the presence of harmonics and non-sinusoidal waveforms, as found in variable frequency drive (VFD) and UPS outputs, switch-mode power supplies, and inverter-driven loads
- **Measurement functions:**
  - AC/DC voltage up to 1000 V
  - AC/DC current (with shunt or external clamp adapter) up to 10 A direct / 600 A with clamp
  - Resistance: 0.1 Ω to 40 MΩ
  - Continuity with audible beeper
  - Diode forward voltage drop
  - Capacitance: 1 nF to 10,000 µF
  - Frequency and duty cycle
  - Temperature via Type-K thermocouple probe (model dependent)
- **Auto-ranging** with manual range override
- **Data hold, max/min, and relative (REL) measurement modes**
- **Backlit display** for use in poorly lit switchrooms and outdoor night work
- **Safety rating:** CAT III 1000 V / CAT IV 600 V — appropriate for primary distribution measurements
- **Low-input impedance mode (Lo-Z)** — eliminates phantom voltage readings on de-energised circuits adjacent to live conductors
- **Durable, drop-tested housing** — survives 1 m drop to concrete

## Applications

- Voltage presence checks before work commences (PTW/LOTO procedures)
- Transformer secondary voltage and symmetry verification
- Battery room DC voltage and charger output measurement
- Control circuit troubleshooting in relay panels and PLCs
- Continuity checking of cable and earth connections
- Harmonic voltage assessment in sensitive electronic load environments
