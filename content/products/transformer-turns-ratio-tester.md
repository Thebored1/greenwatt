# Transformer Turns Ratio Tester (TTR)

## Green Watt Transformer Turns Ratio Test Set

The transformer turns ratio (TTR) test verifies that a transformer's winding ratio matches its nameplate specification and that all tap positions are correct — a fundamental commissioning and maintenance check for power, distribution, and instrument transformers. Incorrect turns ratio can cause metering errors, protection relay maloperation, and voltage regulation problems. Green Watt's TTR tester provides fast, accurate automated ratio measurement for transformers from distribution level to EHV.

## Key Features

- **Automatic ratio measurement** — applies a low-voltage AC excitation to the primary and measures the resulting secondary voltage; calculates the ratio and deviation from nominal automatically
- **Wide ratio range** — measures turns ratios from 0.8 to 10,000 (or higher) covering distribution transformers (11/0.433 kV), power transformers (400/220/132/33 kV), and instrument transformers
- **All tap positions** — tests all on-load tap changer (OLTC) and off-circuit tap changer (OCTC) positions sequentially
- **Phase angle measurement** — measures the phase angle between primary and secondary voltages to verify winding polarity and vector group
- **Vector group verification** — confirms transformer vector group (Dyn11, YNyn0, etc.) by phase relationship measurement
- **Excitation current display** — monitors exciting current during ratio test as an indirect indicator of core and winding condition
- **High accuracy** — ratio error measurement to ±0.1% or better; phase angle to ±1 minute
- **Automatic three-phase testing** — tests all three phases in a single connection setup
- **Built-in printer / USB output** — on-site printed or digital test report with operator input fields
- **Safety protection** — HV detection circuit prevents damage if test leads are connected incorrectly

## Applications

- Commissioning of new power and distribution transformers
- Periodic maintenance testing to detect tap changer problems, shorted turns, or winding damage
- Post-repair verification after transformer rewinding or bushing replacement
- Factory acceptance testing at transformer manufacturing works
- Post-lightning or fault event assessment

## Compliance

Test methodology conforms to IEC 60076-1, IS 2026, and IEEE C57.12.90 requirements for transformer ratio testing.
